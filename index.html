📖 E&E Opti Lab User & Operator Manual

1. Shift Workbench & Rollover Operations

Technicians clock in at shift start, select assigned task cards, click ▶️ Start Test, and record actual cycles or hours completed at shift end. Incomplete tests automatically rollover to the next shift with full progress preserved.

2. Multi-Pack Envisys ET-600 Chambers & Telemetry

Envisys ET-600 chambers support up to 3 packs simultaneously (Slots A, B, C). Clicking an ET-600 tile displays all 3 pack streams with 3 independent Grafana dashboard deep links.

3. Custom SCADA Layout Builder

Click on the floor canvas grid in the SCADA Floor Twin tab to create new equipment blocks. Enter custom Grafana telemetry links for each station.
